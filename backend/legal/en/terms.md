**Version:** 2026-08-22

## 1. Agreement

These Terms of Service (“Terms”) govern access to and use of the BaladiGuard civic reporting platform, including citizen mobile and web apps, staff tools, and related APIs (the “Service”). By creating an account or using the Service, you agree to these Terms.

BaladiGuard is intended for users **16 years of age or older**.

## 2. Controllers and roles

- The **BaladiGuard platform operator** processes citizen accounts, authentication, and platform operations needed to run the Service.
- **Participating municipalities** process municipal ticket records for civic operations (intake, routing, investigation, and resolution) within their jurisdiction.

## 3. The Service

BaladiGuard helps residents submit infrastructure and civic reports (for example location, description, and photos) and helps municipal staff review and resolve those reports. Features may evolve; we may add, change, or discontinue capabilities with reasonable notice where practicable.

## 4. Accounts and authentication

Citizen accounts are phone-first and use one-time passcodes (OTP). You must keep your phone number under your control and not share OTP codes. You are responsible for activity under your authenticated sessions.

Staff accounts are issued by authorized administrators for municipal or platform work only.

## 5. Acceptable use

You must follow the Acceptable Use Policy. You may not abuse the Service, submit fraudulent reports, attempt unauthorized access, or interfere with municipal operations.

## 6. Reports and content

You retain rights in content you submit, and you grant BaladiGuard and participating municipalities a license to use that content as needed to operate civic workflows (including storage, routing, review, and lawful retention of municipal records). Ticket contact snapshots and ownership references may be retained for operational and audit integrity even after account anonymization, as described in the Privacy Policy.

## 7. Privacy

Personal data is handled as described in the Privacy Policy. Contact privacy questions at privacy@baladiguard.app.

## 8. Availability and disclaimers

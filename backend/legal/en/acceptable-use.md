# Acceptable Use Policy

**Version:** 2026-08-22  
**Contact:** privacy@baladiguard.app

## 1. Purpose

BaladiGuard exists to support good-faith civic reporting and municipal response. This Acceptable Use Policy explains what is not allowed on the Service.

## 2. Required age

You must be **16 years of age or older** to use the Service.

## 3. Allowed use

You may submit accurate reports about civic or infrastructure issues in participating areas, track your submissions, and manage your account settings.

## 4. Prohibited conduct

Do not:

- Submit false, misleading, harassing, or abusive reports
- Upload illegal content, or content that exploits or endangers minors
- Share OTP codes, impersonate others, or attempt unauthorized access
- Probe, scan, or disrupt the Service, APIs, or municipal systems
- Use the Service for commercial spam, advertising, or unrelated bulk messaging
- Circumvent rate limits, safety filters, or staff access controls
- Collect or scrape other users’ personal data from the Service

## 5. Photos and location

Only upload photos and location data relevant to the reported issue. Do not deliberately include unnecessary sensitive personal information when it can be avoided.

## 6. Enforcement

We may refuse, remove, or escalate content; suspend accounts; revoke sessions; or cooperate with municipalities and lawful authorities when this policy is violated.

## 7. Reporting concerns

Report abuse or privacy concerns to privacy@baladiguard.app.
